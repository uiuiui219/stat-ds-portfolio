Disclaimer · ข้อชี้แจง
These projects were built as hands-on exercises for specific courses and were assessed on applying the method, not on production readiness. Some points may therefore not fully follow best practice — for example simplified data cleaning, small samples, or model choices driven by the assignment's requirements. They are shared as evidence of coursework, with that caveat.
