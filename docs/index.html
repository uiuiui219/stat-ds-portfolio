F1 Driver Points Prediction

Predicting whether a Formula 1 driver finishes in the points (Top 10) from race-weekend features: driver history, grid, circuit clustering and race-day weather — with SHAP-based interpretation of what actually drives a scoring finish.

ทำนายการเข้าเส้นชัยในกลุ่มคะแนนของนักขับ F1 จากผลงานย้อนหลัง ตำแหน่งออกสตาร์ท สภาพอากาศ และการจัดกลุ่มสนาม พร้อมอธิบายโมเดลด้วย SHAP

How it works ขั้นตอนการทำงาน

  1. Data & feature engineering — results, grids, pit stops and standings from the Ergast F1 API (2019–2024, 2,559 race entries); race-day temperature/rain/wind from Open-Meteo aligned to local race windows; tyre-stress data from Pirelli track data. Driver age, experience, rolling podium/points history, constructor wins and DNF flags — with an explicit data-leakage review (no post-race information).
  2. Circuit clustering — 128 circuits → Gower distance → hierarchical clustering → 5 clusters chosen by Average Silhouette Width; cluster membership becomes a feature.
  3. Modelling — Random Forest vs XGBoost with grid-searched hyperparameters, evaluated with time-series cross-validation (expanding window, 4 folds): train 2019–2023, test = 2024 season.
  4. Interpretation — SHAP (beeswarm + interactions): weather and circuit-cluster features both land in the top 5.
  5. Deployment view — a 3-class podium / scoring / no-points assignment under F1 quota constraints (3 + 7 + 10), presented in Power BI.
